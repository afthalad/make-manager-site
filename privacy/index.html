Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how Make Manager ("the app"), made by Afthal Ahmadh, handles your information. In short: we do not collect your data, we have no server of our own, and we do not track you.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data. The app has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your Make API token

To use the app, you sign in with your own Make API token. The token is saved only in the Keychain on your device. It is never sent to us. Signing out removes it from your device.

Data the app reads from Make

The app talks directly from your device to Make's official API. It reads your name, email, organizations, teams, scenarios and usage numbers so it can show them to you. When you turn a scenario on or off, the app sends that request to Make. This data stays between your device and Make. It is not sent to us or anyone else.

Make handles this data under its own privacy policy. See Make's privacy notice.

Apple

If you choose to share crash reports and app analytics with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may share anonymous crash and usage data with us. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

The app is free and has no in-app purchases right now. If we add purchases later, we will update this policy before they go live.

Children

The app is not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Please check it from time to time.

Contact

Not linked with Make
