Common questions

How do I get a Make API token?
  1. Open Make and go to your Profile.
  2. Open the API tab and tap Add token.
  3. Choose these scopes: organizations:read, teams:read, scenarios:read and scenarios:write.
  4. Copy the token and paste it in the app.
The app says my token stopped working.

The token may be deleted, expired or missing a scope. Make a new token in Make with the four scopes above, then sign in again.

The app says I do not have permission.

Your Make role in that team may not allow this action, or the token is missing a scope. Ask your team admin, or make a new token with all four scopes.

The app says there are too many requests.

Make limits how many requests you can send per minute. The limit depends on your Make plan. Wait a minute and try again.

My company uses its own Make address.

On the sign-in screen, tap Use a custom Make address and enter your company's Make domain.

How do I sign out?

Open the Profile tab and tap Sign Out. This removes your token from the device.
